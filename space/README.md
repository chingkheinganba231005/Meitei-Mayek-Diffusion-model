# Handwritten Meitei Mayek word generation

Type Meitei Mayek words and see them written by hand: in the hand of a test writer, in the
author's own hand, in a random hand, or in yours, from one to eight photos or scans of words
you have written. The generator is the first diffusion model for handwritten Meitei Mayek.
It runs on two CPU cores, so a word takes about 10 to 20 seconds.

{results}

Please do not imitate a person's handwriting without their consent.
